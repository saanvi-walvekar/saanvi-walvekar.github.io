A megapixel count says how many pixels are in the picture. It says nothing about how much light each pixel collected.

**Sensor size.** Twelve megapixels on a large sensor means large pixels, and each one gathers more light. More light means less noise and better detail in shadows. The same twelve megapixels squeezed onto a phone sensor are much smaller.

**The lens.** A sensor can only record what the lens delivers. A soft or small lens blurs detail before it ever reaches the pixels.

**Processing.** Every camera turns raw sensor data into a picture differently: how it sharpens, removes noise, handles colour and combines several exposures. Phones lean on this heavily, which is why two phones with the same sensor can still look different.
